Our Expertise

Specialised Services

Comprehensive care using evidence-based protocols and modern technology.

🛡️

ECG & Echo

Non-invasive cardiac diagnostics including resting ECG, 2D echocardiography, stress echocardiography, and Holter monitoring. These tests provide a detailed picture of your heart's structure, function, and electrical activity — helping detect problems early and guide treatment decisions.

💆

Angiography

Detailed X-ray imaging of the coronary arteries performed in a catheterisation lab to precisely locate and assess blockages. Coronary angiography is the gold standard for diagnosing coronary artery disease and is the essential first step before planning angioplasty or bypass surgery.

🧪

Angioplasty

A minimally invasive catheter-based procedure to open narrowed or blocked coronary arteries, often with stent placement to keep them open. Also used as primary PCI — the fastest and most effective treatment for acute heart attacks, restoring blood flow rapidly to protect the heart muscle.

🧬

Hypertension

Comprehensive evaluation and long-term management of high blood pressure, including 24-hour ambulatory BP monitoring, lifestyle counselling, and personalised medication plans. Effective BP control significantly reduces the risk of stroke, heart attack, and kidney damage over time.

🔍

Heart Failure

Specialised care for patients with heart failure and cardiomyopathy, focusing on symptom control, medication optimisation, and device therapy such as pacemakers and CRT devices. Regular follow-up and early intervention help patients maintain quality of life and avoid hospital admissions.

🌞

Arrhythmia

Diagnosis and treatment of irregular heart rhythms including atrial fibrillation, bradycardia, and tachycardia. Evaluation includes Holter monitoring, EP studies, and where appropriate, pacemaker implantation or catheter ablation to restore a normal, steady heart rhythm.

Cardiac Devices

Pacemaker Options Explained

Understanding the differences helps you make the best decision for your heart health.

🔩

Conventional Pacemaker

Requires a surgical implant. It is the most affordable option but carries a slightly higher infection risk and a small (<10%) chance of worsening heart failure compared to newer alternatives.

⚡

Left Bundle Pacing

The most physiological form of pacing — closely mimics the heart's natural electrical pathway, resulting in very low risk of worsening heart failure. Requires a surgical implant and carries the same infection risk as conventional pacing.

🩺

Leadless Pacemaker

No surgery required — inserted via a catheter approach. Allows early mobilisation with little post-operative pain and a very low risk of infection. Ideal for elderly patients. The only drawback is the higher cost.
